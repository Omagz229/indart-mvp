## 2. Agreed strategic decisions (from Indart planning discussion; not implementation evidence)
- Positioning: Pan-African, premium, globally competitive; transformation over information and community over consumption.
- Christian ethics as an internal organizing principle, not necessarily overt external positioning.
- Initial commercial wedge: Indart Learn. Future Indart Connect (community, professional discovery/marketplace) and Indart Live introduced only through evidence, economics, operations, and mission gates.
- Planned revenue tests: daily/weekly/monthly/annual access, pay-per-video, and hybrid pricing. Protected in-app offline playback is a target requirement.
- Advertising/sponsorship is supplementary, curated, and should not interrupt paid lessons.
- Instructor strategy: upfront, royalty and hybrid options; founding courses proposed, no instructor committed merely by inclusion on a shortlist.
- Financing sequence: negotiate artist terms -> investor proposition -> **secure adequate financing** -> public paid pre-sales -> production and delivery -> evaluate and expand.
- No unfunded artist liability; course pre-sales require defined pricing, fulfillment budget, schedule, customer protections and gated approval.
- Simulation-first, zero-new-cash market research; modeled demand is NOT validated customer demand.
- Investor readiness target: 2027-01-04, not public launch.
